# Request

From Lena Fischer (project manager, Kestrel Ledger), in chat, 2026-11-12:

> The client sent the "contact hub" wish list (ticket OPS-5930) and wants it as one story
> so it's easy to report on. Can you put it on the board? Sam estimates the lot at about 21 points;
> some of it is waiting on the Integration Hub team.

The wish list, as the client wrote it:

1. Customer Success reps see a contact's open support cases on the contact page (newest first,
   10 max, link to all).
2. Contacts marked inactive disappear from the Contacts related list on accounts, but stay
   searchable.
3. A "Last contacted" date on each contact, filled in automatically from logged calls and
   meetings.
4. CS managers can export the contacts of an account to CSV (name, email, phone, role, last
   contacted).
5. Duplicate contacts (same email on the same account) are flagged nightly for a CS manager to
   merge; nothing merges automatically. This needs the Integration Hub's nightly sync, which that
   team ships next month.
